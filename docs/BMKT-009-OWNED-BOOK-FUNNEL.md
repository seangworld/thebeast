# BMKT-009 — Existing published book revenue funnel

## Outcome

BeastAdmin can prepare a governed marketing campaign that connects the existing SEANGWORLD free AI ebook to Sean Gatewood's published Amazon book, *AI for Normal People* (ASIN `B0HFZ1WHLX`). It does not create a new KDP title and does not replace the free offer.

## Owner workflow

1. Open **BeastAdmin → Marketing → Advertising**.
2. Select **Prepare funnel for review** in the Existing-book revenue funnel card.
3. Select the campaign **Owned-book funnel · AI for Normal People · B0HFZ1WHLX**.
4. Approve the exact campaign draft.
5. Review and approve each exact asset separately.

Approval records consent for that revision. It does not publish, schedule, connect Amazon, spend money, or activate the News placement. External release remains a separate governed step.

## Funnel contract

- Acquisition: existing free SEANGWORLD ebook.
- Paid next step: owner-supplied Amazon listing for *AI for Normal People*.
- Retention: invite qualified readers to explore Beast.
- Required owned-product disclosure: `Published by Sean Gatewood. A purchase may earn the author a royalty.`
- Unknowns remain explicit: live Amazon price, formats, availability, ratings, sales, and attribution are not asserted.

The preparation endpoint is owner-only and idempotent by the campaign identity. Repeated preparation selects the existing campaign and creates only missing named assets. External publishing is always reported as disabled.
