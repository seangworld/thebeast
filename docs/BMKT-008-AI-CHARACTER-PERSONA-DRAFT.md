# BMKT-008 — AI character persona draft foundation

BeastMarketing can now capture a consistent fictional female AI influencer brief without creating media or granting identity, renderer, upload, or publishing authority.

## Current production strategy

- FacelessReels remains the current production engine.
- BeastMarketing owns discovery, evidence-backed scripting, calendar/cadence, approvals, handoffs, analytics, and optimization.
- The custom Revision 7 / Shotstack renderer remains paused. This increment makes no Shotstack call and consumes no rendering credits.

## Draft contract

An owner may save a character name, archetype, audience, visual direction, voice direction, and allowed topics. The persisted profile is always `future_character`, inactive, and has no bound face, likeness, or voice asset. Its provenance records identity approval, disclosure approval, asset binding, and publishing authorization as false.

Every future appearance is required to disclose: “AI-generated character; not a real person.” The draft also prohibits impersonation. These fields prepare an owner decision; they do not constitute that decision.

## Remaining gates

Before any image, voice, FacelessReels handoff, private upload, or publication uses the character, the owner must approve the exact identity and name, visual and voice design, disclosure presentation, source/rights provenance, platform/channel scope, and activation. Provider terms and current platform synthetic-media requirements must be checked at that time.
