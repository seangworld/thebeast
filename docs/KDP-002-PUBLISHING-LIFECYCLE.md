# KDP-002 Publishing Lifecycle

This increment turns the KDP candidate queue into a gated preparation workflow. An eligible scored candidate can receive a structured publication brief, the owner can approve that brief, production can advance through drafting and quality review, and a complete evidence checklist is required before the package can become ready for owner approval.

The evidence gate requires the manuscript, interior, cover, metadata, pricing, originality review, rights review, factual review, and AI disclosure preparation. Missing evidence fails closed. A score below 55 cannot advance to brief preparation.

Owner approval does not submit or publish anything. Amazon sign-in, account changes, terms, ISBN decisions, advertising spend, submission, and publication remain separate owner-controlled external actions.
