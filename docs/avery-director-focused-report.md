## Causes and no-cost corrections

Avery repeatedly put ordinary plan approval, information obtained by the proposed tool, or future unrelated decisions there. Safe prose thus became unusable under the actual validator. This is a material assessment-to-production limitation; not grounds to remove approval gates or attribute every refusal solely to base-model reasoning. Existing production instructions already explain these field semantics. They were not changed or silently substituted during this test.

**Model reasoning / tool interpretation:** Conditional cash work was encoded in purpose/dependency prose although the runner cannot evaluate that condition automatically. Recovery added unsupported contradictions/extra prerequisites. Optional unavailable vendor evidence blocked a useful cash review. Correct reimbursement classification, permission refusal, cancellation and evidence humility are positive narrow observations; the missing downstream executions leave correction, recovery and post-result verification competence unproven.

**Output / evaluator reliability:** The output limit truncated one high-reasoning response. Its complete reported usage was reconciled; it was not retried or repaired. The first runner conservatively paused. A no-cost correction allowed only the next unconsumed scheduled turn, rebuilding the unchanged recovery fixture from preserved actual state, retaining all eight prior attempts and their reservations. Unknown usage still halts. Independent permission withdrawal is recorded even if initial approval fails. All frozen scenario/prompt/schema hashes and exact initial request hashes remain unchanged.
