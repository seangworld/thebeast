# BeastEducation product direction

BeastEducation is the member’s education and career planning product, centered on a long-term Guidance Counselor relationship and supported by the released Riley Chen AI Tutor and Homework Helper. The authoritative current definitions are [BE-201-EDUCATION-CAREER-INTELLIGENCE.md](./BE-201-EDUCATION-CAREER-INTELLIGENCE.md) and [BEASTEDUCATION_AI_TUTOR.md](./BEASTEDUCATION_AI_TUTOR.md).

The Guidance Counselor learns the member’s education, employment, military experience, training, credentials, skills, interests, constraints, and goals over time. It identifies the gap between the present position and an intended destination, researches current credible options, compares alternatives, and maintains a realistic member-approved roadmap.

There is no separate active BeastLearning product. BeastEducation includes Riley's bounded tutoring, homework help/review, examples, practice questions, and short quizzes. Riley does not replace the Guidance Counselor or activate the older course catalog, lesson engine, diagnostic-placement, mastery-progression, generalized instructional-content generation, or learning-game systems; those systems remain on hold. Historical data remains preserved and compatibility routes may redirect safely.
