# KDP-001 Factory Foundation

This increment establishes the reusable, provider-neutral core for the approved KDP publishing factory. It ranks candidate publications, enforces a durable lifecycle, keeps the owner's low-price strategy specific to eligible eBooks, calculates print recommendations from current minimum/cost inputs, and prevents incomplete packages from reaching owner approval or submission.

It does not sign in to Amazon, upload or publish a title, accept terms, select an ISBN, spend money, or grant an external integration authority. Marketplace limits and royalty terms are inputs that must be refreshed from current official KDP rules before submission. The completed package remains explicitly owner-gated.

The owner-only BeastMarketing Publishing workspace now persists and scores candidates. KDP-002 extends it with a structured brief, lifecycle controls, quality evidence, and a separate owner approval handoff without coupling preparation to an Amazon account connection.
